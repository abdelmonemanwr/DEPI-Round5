Eager Loading:
    var employee = dbContext.Employees.First(e=>e.Id==5).Include(e=>e.Department.Name);

Excplict Loading:
    var employee = dbContext.Employees.FirstOrDefault(e=>e.Id==5);
    dbContext.Entry(employee).Reference(e=>e.Department).Load(); // one related data

    var department = dbContext.Department.FirstOrDefault(d=>d.Id==5);
    dbContext.Entry(department ).Collection(e=>e.Employees).Load();  // Many related data
